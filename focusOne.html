The Discipline of Saying No

1. Dump without building
When inspiration hits, offload it into the top bar immediately. Do not start a repo. Do not design an icon. Do not write a script. Free your mind.
2. Survive the 48-hour quarantine
Ideas are locked for 48 hours. Most ideas feel brilliant only because dopamine makes them effortless in your head. When the chemical high fades, 90% are not worth doing.
3. Exactly one active focus
You are permitted only one active project. You cannot switch, pause, or multitask. You must either legitimately ship it or consciously kill it.
