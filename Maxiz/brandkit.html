Brand brief

Positioning, audience, and design direction

What Maxiz is

A business consulting service for small and medium businesses (SMBs) in Western Sydney, offering AI, website, social media, legal, and accounting support under one roof — one partner instead of five separate vendors.

Audience

Small and medium business owners in Western Sydney — time-poor, juggling multiple functions themselves, looking for a single point of contact rather than five separate providers.

Key message

One consulting partner, five essential services. "Less is more" — simplify who you rely on to run your business.

Tone of voice

Professional but approachable — not corporate-stiff. Speaks to a busy owner, not a boardroom. Plain language, short sentences, no jargon.

Design principles

  • Restraint over decoration — every element earns its place or gets cut, not padded
  • One call to action, said the same way everywhere: "Message us on WhatsApp"
  • Plain serif wordmark, clean sans-serif body text, one accent color used sparingly
  • Generous white space, minimal borders, no gradients or drop shadows
  • Works identically in print (card, flyer) and digital (site, social)

Logo & brand kit

Wordmark, color palette, and typography

Logo

A plain serif wordmark — deliberately undecorated. No underline, no icon, no flourish that needs explaining. The restraint of the mark itself carries "less is more," rather than a decorative detail standing in for the idea.
