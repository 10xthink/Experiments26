A brief history of the heart

Love stories are as old as storytelling.

Romantic storytelling reaches back through ancient poetry, myths, epics and folktales. The modern romance novel grew from changing ideas about marriage, individual choice and women's lives. Today the field spans historical romance, romantic comedy, fantasy, queer love stories and more. Genre romance commonly centres a developing romantic relationship and offers an emotionally satisfying, optimistic ending; this library also includes adjacent stories about love that may end differently.
